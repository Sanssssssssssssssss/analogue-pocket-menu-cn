Generated Intel/Altera IP keeps its original notices. The stable GB/GBC source is included under `core/src/`; its compiler version, input hashes and binary checksum are documented in [BUILD](docs/BUILD.md). The multi-system development preview includes additional bitstreams. Their corresponding prepared source snapshots, licenses and build instructions accompany the preview as described in [preview sources](docs/PREVIEW-SOURCES.md). These candidates have separate hardware-acceptance status. Distribute the source companion together with the binary preview. Licensed Quartus tools and simulation models are obtained separately.
